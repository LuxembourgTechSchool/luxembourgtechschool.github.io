As a young student, you are probably not yet in charge of the household finances, but you are still affected by the financial and law systems around you. You might have heard before about liability, contractual clauses or financial risk, but would you know how to evaluate the conditions of a loan and understand what a contract commits you to?

Many students are introduced to the finance world through social media. Investment tips and success stories appear in their feeds, and AI tools can answer almost any question they have about money. This exposure can help spark their interest in learning more about how finance works. Nonetheless, misleading information can remain difficult for them to recognise.

Level F-1 is a 5-month program in which LTS Coaches will guide you through the ways finance, law and AI influence one another. You will learn the basics of personal finances, investments, crypto, AI applied to finance and more. At the end of the program, you work on your project and pitch in front of industry experts!

Exploring finance and law can be fun, and building knowledge in these areas from a young age can help you make more informed decisions and avoid common financial and legal pitfalls later in life.
