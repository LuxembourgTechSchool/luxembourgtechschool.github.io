The Digital Brain is an immersive, interactive experience that was researched, imagined, designed, and built from scratch over the course of a year by four students from the Luxembourg Tech School: Juliana, Vasileia, Gabriel, and Punyabhalan, guided by LTS coaches Sara Kaiser and Julia Boes.

Blending science fiction with emerging neurotechnologies and supported by immersive storytelling and performance design, this project became a bold experimental learning journey and an invitation for public audiences to explore what it means to be human in an age of thinking machines.

As four curious young minds, the students co-created this experience from the ground up, combining creative writing, technical development, and futures thinking. Together, they explored how brain-computer interfaces are evolving from science fiction into science fact, with the goal of inviting audiences into a world where human consciousness and neurotechnology meet, sparking reflection on the choices we face in our rapidly changing digital age.

The Digital Brain project is supported by the Luxembourg National Research Fund (FNR).
