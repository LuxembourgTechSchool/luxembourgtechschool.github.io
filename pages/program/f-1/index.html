Finance will not be a secret for you

This shorter program aims at giving you the required knowledge and confidence to tackle the real life. Be it about managing your day-to-day personal finances or understanding contracts before signing them, those are skills that are easy to learn and can help you set up your future.

Just like all LTS programs, you work in teams and on real projects that are showcased in front of industry experts.

Did you just click on: "Yes, I agree..." ?

How many privacy policies, terms and conditions or other contracts have you read in detail? Probably not that many. You overall trusted the other party, right? This program focuses on the topic of law, and will guide you through different types of contracts, what to pay attention to and what your rights are.

Using the latest technology, you will be equipped to quickly analyze and understand documents before signing them. Be careful, you might be surprised how certain companies are using your data.
