Future Digital Leaders Program

This epic program is for all 12-19 years old students who are passionate about the digital world and eager to learn and apply technology in a real business and creative context.

We offer a 3 year program. You can start with Level G0, Level One or Level Up, depending on your age. Each level is divided into up to 3 modules, each centered on one specific domain.

At the end of each course, you have the opportunity to present your project to the real world!

The program below is a preview of the cool topics that are covered. There won't be much theory (a little is always useful). It is filled with practical activities, exercices and you work on your own project!

During Level G0 you discover the creative side of coding and how to talk to machines. During Level One and Level Up, you learn a lot about teamwork, software development, entrepreneurship and even upgrade your presentation skills by multiple factors.

It won't be easy, but at the end you will have created many awesome projects, will have learned a ton of new things and, most importantly, while having a lot of fun.

Future Leaders in Finance, Law & AI

Most people learn how money and the law work far too late, once the costly mistakes are already made. This program flips that. We equip 15-19 year olds with the financial, legal and AI skills to make smart decisions, spot opportunities and stay ahead long before entering the workforce.

Following the LTS methodology, you learn the basics of personal finances, investments, crypto, AI applied on finance and more, and you work on your own project.

At the end of the program,you present your project to the real world, in front of industry experts!

The financial and legal world affects everyone. This program gives you a head start in understanding and navigating the real world, all while having a lot of fun.
