Level G0 is your starting point at LTS: a creative, hands-on first encounter with coding, computers, robots and emerging technologies.

From the very first minute, you are creating, not just watching. You build animated graphics, interactive projects, small games and your own webpage. You see your code come to life on screen straight away, which makes it genuinely fun.

Enter the world of Coding

Technology is creative and interdisciplinary. Whether you are a future designer, researcher, storyteller, engineer, or still figuring out what you love, there is a place for you here.

It is a safe space to explore. You work in a small group with experienced LTS Digital Coaches and Student Coaches (recent LTS graduates). There is always someone to help and plenty of room to experiment at your own pace. At Level G0, you do not compete, you tinker, create and grow more confident week by week.

Activate all your senses

Over the year, you will gain a real first understanding of how code and computers work and, just as importantly, the confidence and curiosity to keep going.

You leave Level G0 with the ability to create with technology rather than only consume it, and to think critically about the digital world around you. You explore a fast-changing field without feeling intimidated by it, which will support you in school and life far beyond this one program.

Jump-start your digital journey

Level G0 is a natural first step into the wider LTS journey. The independent, project-based way of working at the heart of Level One and Level Up is introduced here gently and playfully, through small projects and mini-challenges.

Students who complete Level Go build up their confidence and digital skills faster. You bring the curiosity. We give you the tools, challenges and support to start shaping your future.
