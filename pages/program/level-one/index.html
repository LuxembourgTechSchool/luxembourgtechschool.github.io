Create your own Game

No matter your skill level in technology, you start with a few highly interactive and practical lessons helping you dive head first into a new technology. Be it game development, artificial intelligence or financial technologies, we teach you the basics and you directly move onto building your own projects.

The LTS unique project selection methodology ensures that the best ideas are democratically selected by the students. That way, selected ideas have the highest potential to become a successful project.

Multiply your success by working in a team

Year after year, the core skill that students learn and improve is teamwork. Working with new people, learning from each other, and building a project together is a key skill for success. The compounding effect of a great team is simply undeniable.

From the first day, you will be working in teams and get personalised coaching. Your coach is not your teacher, but your extra team member, there to support you and your team as you move forward.

Get a 5 year head start on technology

5 years before LLMs became mainstream, LTS students were already learning to build their own models. From AI-powered applications, up to machine learning algorithms and generative models, LTS students have been experimenting, playing and building with these technologies.

Becoming a digital leader also means being able to understand, build and use the latest and upcoming technologies. We can't predict the future, but we can prepare for it.
