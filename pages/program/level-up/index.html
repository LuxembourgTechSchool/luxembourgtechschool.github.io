Dive into advanced technologies

What is coming in the next 5-10 years? Robots? Brain-Computer Interfaces? Quantum Computing? Those are the subjects that we tackle, discover and work with. Not only that, we equip students with the required skills and knowledge to build confidence with difficult subjects like cybersecurity.

During the different classes, you work with various autonomous robots, drones and sensor equipment. As with other programs, you work on your own project, and will have even more opportunities to participate in competitions, events and hackathons.

Up your level, Up your options

LTS is more than classes and events. It is filled with professional opportunities that help you grow and learn. For example, many of the students become coaches themselves as part of a student job. Some take the lead to manage parts of social media, others take on bigger tech projects.

Being able to put Luxembourg Tech School in your resume will definitely help you stand out when applying for jobs or university.
